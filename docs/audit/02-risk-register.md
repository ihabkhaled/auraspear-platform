# AUDIT 02 — Risk Register

> Honest accounting of known risks, debt, and deferred work in the
> **AuraSpear Platform** monorepo as of the foundation + hardening milestones.
> Every entry is traceable to a source file in the repo. Source of truth:
> [`docs/audit/FINAL_REPORT.md`](./FINAL_REPORT.md) and the cited files.

**Severity:** how much it can hurt if left unaddressed (Low / Medium / High).
**Status:** `Accepted` (known + tolerated for now), `Deferred` (tracked for a
follow-up milestone), `Pending` (will be exercised but hasn't been yet).

## Notes

- **CI gating posture:** `typecheck` + `build` are **hard gates** (both green
  across 5/5 packages and 2/2 apps); `lint`, `format`, and `test` are advisory
  (`continue-on-error`) until the lint debt in R1 is cleared. This is deliberate
  and documented — see `.github/workflows/ci.yml` and `FINAL_REPORT.md` §8.
- Several R-items (R1, R4) are the same deferrals tracked in
  `FINAL_REPORT.md` §5 and §8 "Still genuinely deferred"; this register
  restates them as discrete, owned risks with a severity and next action.
