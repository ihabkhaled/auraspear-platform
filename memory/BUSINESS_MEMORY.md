# BUSINESS_MEMORY

Stable business truths. Full detail in `docs/business/`.

- **One-liner**: AuraSpear is an AI-first SOC platform that unifies SIEM alerts,
  cases, threat hunting, intelligence enrichment, SOAR automation, and AI
  investigation workflows in a multi-tenant workspace for modern security teams
  and MSSPs.
- **Who buys it**: SOC teams, MSSPs (multi-tenant is core), security-conscious
  startups, and security/compliance leaders who need lower MTTR and less alert
  fatigue without stitching together many point tools.
- **Who uses it (personas)**: global/tenant admins, SOC managers, Tier-1 and
  Tier-2 analysts, threat hunters, detection engineers, incident commanders,
  compliance officers, platform engineers. (Code roles: GLOBAL_ADMIN,
  PLATFORM_OPERATOR, TENANT_ADMIN, SOC_ANALYST_L2, THREAT_HUNTER,
  SOC_ANALYST_L1, EXECUTIVE_READONLY.)
- **Why it exists**: SOC tooling is fragmented; analysts drown in alerts. AuraSpear
  unifies the workflow end-to-end and adds AI-assisted investigation that is
  attributable, auditable, and approval-gated.
- **Differentiators**: AI woven through investigation (not bolted on); multi-tenant
  RBAC; connector-driven integration; query-driven operational dashboards;
  end-to-end SOC coverage; auditable, approval-gated AI.
- **Product modules**: alerts, cases, incidents, hunts, intel/IOC enrichment,
  detection rules, connectors, dashboards/analytics, reporting, RBAC/tenant admin,
  and the AI subsystem (chat, findings, memory, agents, orchestrator).
